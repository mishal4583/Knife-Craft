## Levels 24 to 91: what the player has to do and can play at every level

From Level 20 all eight mini-games are open, so **every level from 24 to 91 has the same list**. Only the difficulty step of each game changes.

**Daily duties (the next service cannot open until each one that is waiting is done; a hired Prep Cook, or a rewarded ad skip, can do them):**
1. Check the delivery: when a delivery basket is waiting.
2. Fridge Sort: when checked food is waiting to be put away.
3. Stock the shelves: when a crate of supplies is waiting.
4. Fridge Check: when a "throw out spoiled food" or "wrap the fridge" chore is waiting.
5. Sauce Station: one round every business day (a duty since Level 10).
6. Car Park: one lot every business day.

**Free play (never required, no reward):** Dessert Run, Unload the Van, and Fridge Check practice.

**Also every level:** the cleaning chores (kitchen, dining room, restroom) and the dishes at the sink, when the service leaves them.

**Difficulty step each game is on (1 = easiest; the last number in each column heading below is how many steps the game has):**

Check the delivery 5, Fridge Sort 5, Stock the shelves 5, Sauce Station 4, Fridge Check 5, Car Park 5, Unload the Van 13, Dessert Run 6.
