## 2. What still makes the loop level-centric (the Phase 1 gap)

Read from the source at `ae37b1d`:

1. **The Kitchen's main card is a level card.** It shows the level title, "ready to prepare · about $X" and a
   **Prepare / Replay** button that calls `onSelectLevel(todayLevel.id)` (`Kitchen.tsx` ≈ L506–541). Below it an
   expandable list of levels each has its own Prepare / Replay (`Kitchen.tsx` ≈ L820–852).
2. **Roll-on stops in three cases** (`App.tsx` `finishCampaignLevel`: `done.first && !done.story && next`): a story
   card, a replay, and the last level. After a story card the player lands on the Kitchen and must tap Prepare again.
3. **A reload loses the roll-on** (`rollOnRef` is in memory only). The player lands on the Kitchen and taps Prepare.
   Nothing is lost, but the service visibly "stops".
4. **"Level N" is printed on the service path itself:** `PreServiceCheck.tsx` header "📆 Day {day} · Level {n}"
   (≈ L293) and per-service "Lv {n}" (≈ L502); `ServiceCheckLayer.tsx` "↩ Back to Level {n} Pre-Service Check"
   (≈ L104); `ClosingTime.tsx` "✓ {name} · Level {n}" (≈ L90); `DailyMaintenance.tsx` service list (≈ L155, L177);
   `RestaurantAttentionPanel.tsx` "Lv {n} restock" (≈ L43, L89).
5. **Unlock copy everywhere says "opens at Level N"** (Kitchen tabs, Menu, Staff, Market Second Floor, Cleanliness,
   Deliveries, City Ranking, Business Dashboard "Level x / 148"). That is the Phase 7 milestone question, not
   Phase 1: these screens are off the critical path.

## 3. Phase 1 goal and the smallest customer-order slice

**Goal:** from the Kitchen, one button opens the restaurant; customers keep arriving and are served until the day
closes or something genuinely needs the player (stock, a story card). The player never picks a level, and no screen
on that path names one. Levels stay as the internal schedule (D7): what a service teaches, pays and unlocks.

**Smallest slice (P1-A), no save change, no economy change:**

1. **One "Open for service" button.** The Kitchen's main card becomes a service card: the next uncompleted
   level's dish emoji and "next customer: {dish}" (+ the existing `payText` "about $X"), and one button that calls
   the existing `onSelectLevel(nextLevel.id)`. The same function, the same Pre-Service Check / Closing Time / opening
   gates. No new money or service code.
2. **Replay leaves the main card.** Replays stay possible from the Order Board (still a screen, reachable from the
   Kitchen, off the main path). Replay rules unchanged (pays nothing).
3. ~~**Roll on after a story card.**~~ (dropped, decision P3) When `done.story` is true, keep a pending roll-on and start the next level when
   the story overlay closes (the overlay already has one completion path, CLAUDE.md §9). Still only after a
   *first* completion, still never past the last level (the endless restaurant stays its own door).
4. **Roll on after a reload.** Derive it instead of storing it: if the save has a completed level whose successor
   is unlocked and not started, the Kitchen's service card is the "open for service" state. No field; the player
   taps once (a reload is a natural break; auto-starting a service on load would surprise the player and could open
   a Pre-Service Check unprompted).
5. **Take "Level N" off the service path** (list in §2.4): show the day and the service name/dish instead
   ("📆 Day 3 · Lunch"). Keep the level number in the ⓘ InfoTip text where a player may want it, and in data-attributes
   the tests use.

**Out of P1-A (later slices):**
- **P1-B (Phase 2):** a visible customer queue and an arrival schedule (D3's second half): "2 waiting" on the floor,
  deterministic arrivals by restaurant capacity, orders drawn from **unlocked and stocked** menu items instead of the
  level's rolled tickets. This changes what is earned and so needs a new frozen baseline — not part of Phase 1.
- **Phase 7:** "opens at Level N" copy → milestones.

## 5. Save compatibility

- **No new save field, no migration.** P1-A reads `levelProgress` (`completedLevelIds`, `highestUnlockedLevelId`,
  `currentLevelId`) as it is; the reload case (§3.4) is derived from it.
- **Old saves:** every save shape the migration already accepts (no `campaignVersion`, v2 = 150 levels renumbered,
  v3) lands on the same "next uncompleted level", so the service card shows the right dish. The ~60 e2e seeds with
  `campaignVersion: 2` keep exercising that path (audit D9).
- **Mid-service reload:** unchanged — the in-progress service is rebuilt from `levelProgress.tickets` / `paidOrders`
  / `menuGuests` exactly as today; nothing is paid twice.
- **Pending table cash** (`business.pendingCash`) and `settings.autoService` are untouched.
- **Bridge:** `level_started` / `level_completed` keep firing per internal level; no analytics rename (stable names, CLAUDE.md §2).
