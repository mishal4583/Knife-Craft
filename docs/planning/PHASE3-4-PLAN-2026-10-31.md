## 0. First: the table cash (what went wrong, what is now true)

What I got wrong: serving the next order collected the cash that was already on the table
(`withPendingCash` settled the old pile first), the cash was a line of text on the served card, and there was no cash
picture on the tables or the map. That removed the thing you asked me to keep.

Now (rule changed 2026-10-31, "let it pile up like other idle games until the user collects it"):

- The cash **lies on the tables** as bundles of bills with its amount, in the dining room (the arrival card) and on the
  **restaurant map** by the front tables. One tap on a pile collects everything. The Kitchen's "on the table · Collect"
  and the Pre-Service Check's "Collect $X from the tables" do the same.
- It **piles up**: the next order, leaving the service, closing the game, reloading or a level rolling on collect nothing.
  Orders of one pay kind share one pile (one ledger entry when collected); menu guests' revenue is its own pile.
- **Auto never collects it.** It cuts and serves; the cash stays for the player.
- **Closing:** the table cash must be collected **before the night's chores and the count**: the Collect button is at the
  top of Closing Time, every chore (and the count) shows "💵 First" until it is in the till, and Close for the night waits.
- Tests: `pending-cash-qa` (S4–S4d, new rule), `table-cash-qa` (new), `party-served-together-qa` C3 (rule changed, updated),
  `prep-cook-auto-qa` A4/A5, `service-flow-qa` D3; browser `tablecash.mjs` (7/7, new), `prepcook.mjs`, `cashflow.mjs`,
  `sharedprep.mjs`, `cashreload.mjs`.
- The save keeps its shape: `business.pendingCash` (existing field) gets an optional `more` list for a second pay kind.
  Old saves read as before.

### 1.1 What the measurement says (completionist simulation, order earnings per service)

- **Reach — nothing is a wall.** Every knife and board costs 0.3–2.2 services of the completionist's income at
  Levels 10–20 ($1,004 a service). All of them open with the Market at Level 5.
- **Ladders.** Knives, boards and fridges rise with their unlock level. The kitchen tiers do not ($60,000, $12,000, $18,000,
  $24,000, $29,000): the known D8 exception, pinned by a test so a change is seen.
- **The items do not pay back in money.** The completionist who buys every item ($58,430 of knives, boards, Blacksmith,
  fridges, helpers) ends with $14,578 more because of them, a return of **0.25×**. They are power and quality items.
- **The kitchen tiers do not pay back in money either.** Their quality share of the order earnings is $6–$29 a service
  against $12,000–$60,000 (950–9,500 services). They are restoration milestones that also add quality and menu seats.

I changed **no price** (an economy change needs your decision). Decision D8-b below.

### 2.1 Decision needed (D10): who automates what?

Today one hired **Prep Cook** unlocks the whole Auto chain. Your Phase 4 list names four roles. Options:

1. **Keep it as is** (Prep Cook = cut + cook + serve chain). Simple; the Line Cook and Server stay wage-paying requirements.
2. **One role per job:** the Prep Cook cuts, the **Line Cook** does the oven step (otherwise the player does it by hand
   after the cook's cut), the **Server** serves and seats guests (otherwise the player taps Serve). Auto then needs all three.
   More depth, but a new rule for what each plate scores and when the oven step appears.
   I recommend option 2 only if you want Auto to be an end-game goal; I have not changed it.
