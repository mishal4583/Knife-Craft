## TASK: paint the parts of a game's Mini-games screen (art only, no code)

**Reference:** the attached mock-up. Match it as closely as you can: its glossy mobile-game cartoon style, colours (deep red, royal purple, warm
gold, cream), rounded gold rims, soft highlights and lighting. The mock-up is a flat picture. Your job is to paint its **parts as separate files**,
so the game can put them together at any screen size.

**Save to:** `Mini-games Sheet Assets/v2/`, using exactly the file names and sizes below.

### Rules for every picture

1. **One thing per file.** No collages, sheets, mock-ups or labels.
2. **PNG with a real alpha channel.** Everything outside the object is fully transparent: never white, grey or a painted checkerboard.
   Only the two `festival_scene` files fill their whole canvas.
3. **No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks**, including on signs, tents, ribbons, pills and buttons. The mock-up's scrambled sign
   writing must not appear. The only exceptions are the white **P** on the parking sign and the white **X** on the close button.
4. Leave about **12 px of empty space** on every side, and crop nothing (except where a file says it must reach the edge).
5. One light for everything: warm, from the top. Crisp edges, no glow halo or fringe around the cut-out.
6. Parts that the game stretches (cards, ribbons, pills, buttons, tabs) must have a **plain, even middle**. Keep ornaments at the corners and
   ends.
