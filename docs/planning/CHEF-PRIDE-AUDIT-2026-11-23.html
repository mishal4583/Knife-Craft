C. Implemented correctly

  • The chefRecord model with clamping and a worst-case size of about 2.7 KB.
  • The strict-improvement news rule and the near-miss rule.
  • All 49 rival chefs, the Progress line and the celebration wiring; no ranking maths changed.
  • Six identities and 24 badge-only title achievements with a derived title.
  • The five dares with deterministic daily choice and an opt-in flow that persists across a reload.
  • No changes to economy, wallet, ledger, ads, the Bridge or the save manager; no clipboard, network or visibility use added.
  • A measurement document with a QA guard.

D. Partially implemented

  • Plate recording works in campaign, batch, daily and endless modes, but not in business-service mode (F1).
  • Shift card: tiles and accuracy lines exist, but spoilage and the performance tiers are missing.
  • Titles: the inputs for Customer Favourite, Restaurateur, Speed and Cleanliness are narrower than the plan.
  • Paige rival beats: permanent rather than transient, no Later, different seen-markers.
  • Dares: progress is not on the shift card, and Zero Waste has a loophole.
  • Perfect-cut tracking: correct in code, never proven on a real plate.

E. Skipped

  • bestPlate in the record.
  • Spoiled and 0 wasted on the shift card, and the Excellent Work, Chef tiers.
  • The respect rival line and the cuisine/district template path.
  • The rival_beat_shown event name.
  • The REFERENCE.md entries.
  • InfoTip use for longer explanations.
  • Real-play verification: Level Complete card, rival pass, title and prestige celebrations, perfect-cut counting, Zero Waste, Clean Freak, the dares-ladder unlock.
  • A missed-day browser scenario (the unit test covers it).
