## IN PROGRESS — cleaning mini-game rework (not finished, not tested)

Goal: realistic dirt in the kitchen, dining area and restroom, and a satisfying
"dopamine loop" while cleaning.

Written so far (type-checks, lints clean, NOT yet run in a browser):

- `src/game/restaurant/cleaningDirt.ts` — pure, seeded stain generator. Real stain kinds
  (sauce splash, smear, coffee ring, stuck-on crust, grease streaks, crumbs, scuffs,
  footprints, drink puddle, limescale, water spots, tile grime) placed on the room art's
  surfaces; each has a tool and a toughness (crumbs go in one sweep, grease needs a few
  rubs); scrub amount, cleaned share, streak and milestone helpers.
- `src/components/kc/business/cleaning/dirtArt.ts` — paints each stain on its own sprite
  (wet middles, dried rims, droplets, gloss, shadows, tread marks, grout dirt).
- `src/components/kc/business/cleaning/cleaningFx.ts` — particles: suds, dust, drops,
  scraped flakes, sparkles and a shine ring when a stain is gone. Off under Reduced Motion.
- `src/game/AudioManager.ts` — `playScrub` (wet / dry / brush), `playSparkle(step)` (rises
  with the streak), `playSpotless`; all behind the Sound setting.
- `src/components/kc/business/AreaCleaningPrototype.tsx` — rewritten on the above: stains
  fade where you rub, tool follows the finger, streak chip, 25/50/75% cheers, room brightens
  as it gets cleaner, "stains left" counter, finale cascade + "Spotless!" banner with time and
  best streak, then the task completes as before (`onComplete`). Timers, frame loop and
  particles are cleaned up on close.
- `src/styles.css` — `kc-clean-*` animations, disabled under Reduced Motion.

Still to do:
