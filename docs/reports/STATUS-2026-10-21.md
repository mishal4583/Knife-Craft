## DONE — cleaning mini-game rework (finished 2026-10-22)

"dopamine loop" while cleaning. Finished and tested on 2026-10-22; details in
`docs/REFERENCE.md` §5 `cleaning-dirt-qa`.

- Stains sit on each tier's real surfaces (`KITCHEN_GEO`, `DINING_TABLES`,
  `RESTROOM_GEO` in `cleaningDirt.ts`, measured from the six tiers' art), about 30%
  bigger so they read on a phone.
- Proper tools: 🧽 Sponge, 🧴 Spray & cloth, 🧹 Broom, plus drawn icons for the Mop,
  Grill scraper, Toilet brush and Squeegee (`cleaning/CleaningToolIcon.tsx`,
  `cleaning/toolLook.ts`); the tool in the hand follows the finger.
- A guide ring shows where to rub (again after 3.5 s idle); the wrong tool says
  "Use the X here" and lights the right button.
- Layout: header, the room, then tools and progress below it (nothing covers the dirt);
  fits 320×568; buttons ≥ 48 px.
- Tests: `scripts/cleaning-dirt-qa.mts` (pure module) and `tools/e2e/cleaningfeel.mjs`
  (9 checks); `restaurantcleanliness.mjs` 9/9.

No save fields, money or ledger changes in the cleaning work.
