## Narrative and completion rewards

- The original Level 100 story finale moves to the campaign midpoint, Level 75.
- The Last Page and Family Legacy remain one-time completion rewards at Level 150. The legacy milestone ID stays campaign-complete, so existing claim and ledger history still prevents a second payment.
- Original 150-level and 200-level milestones move to new Levels 125 and 140. Knife, board, kitchen-upgrade, café and restaurant thresholds are remapped through the same retained-source threshold function.
- Old Level 250 completion marks every new level complete, retaining Endless access. Partial saves transfer completion only for retained levels or exact recipe-pool/order/batch matches. Wallet, ledger, inventory, equipment ownership, story mask and milestone claim history remain untouched by the campaign-progress migration.
