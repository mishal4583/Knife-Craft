# KnifeCraft — Gameplay from Level 1 to 250

How the game unfolds, stage by stage: what you do, what gets introduced, and
what you can buy. Numbers come from the game's own data (the restaurant
build that ships on Pages and Playgama).

---

## The core loop (every level)

1. **Kitchen** → tap **Prepare** on Today's Order.
2. **Pre-Service Check** (from Level 15) — do you have the food, plates,
   staff? Restock in the Market if not.
3. **Cook** — cut, peel, dice, smash… the dish's orders on the board.
4. **Plating** → the chef takes the plate → a short **cooking clip**.
5. **Knife Report** — your cut quality → **Serve** → money.
6. **Finish Level** → Level Complete (order pay + completion reward, city
   ranking if you climbed).

A restaurant **day** = **Lunch + Dinner** (two levels), plus **Breakfast**
from Level 51. Until Level 20 a day just turns ("☀️ Day 2 begins" on Level
Complete); from Level 21 each day opens with its card and ends with
**Closing Time**.

## Stage by stage

### Levels 1–10 · Opening the Restaurant  *(stage: Apprentice Restaurant)*
- **Story:** the opening cinematic; you reopen the family restaurant.
- **Learn to cut:** slice, peel, halve (Levels 1–5 teach everything with a
  ghost hand + how-to card), **dice** at L6, **smash** at L9.
- **The restaurant opens up one piece at a time** (bottom bar): Kitchen from
  the start, **Inventory at L3**, **Market at L7** (a look at knives and
  boards), **Progress at L10**, **Restaurant at L11**. Locked ones show
  🔒 "Lv N".
- **Grandma** says a line on every Level Complete up to Level 15.
- **The day:** days pass quietly ("☀️ Day 2 begins").
- **Nothing to stock yet** — just cook.
- **Milestone L10:** *"The room comes back."* — a big card.
- **Unlocks at L10:** the first things to buy: Santoku knife ($350), Maple
  Board ($300), optional.

### Levels 11–20 · Italian Kitchen  *(stage: Opening Menu → Pantry at L15)*
- **L11 — Your menu** opens with 4 dishes. **Menu guests** start: 1 per
  service (optional extra orders that pay the dish's menu price).
- **L15 — Ingredient stock:** orders now use real food from your fridge.
  The **Pre-Service Check** appears; buy ingredients in the **Market**
  (¼ lb steps, bulky produce sold whole). Menu grows to 6 dishes.
  Paring Knife unlocks ($500).
- **L16 — Rings** technique.
- **L20:** Herb Garden Board ($500), **Prep Assistant** helper ($3,000,
  one-time, no wages). Menu 8 dishes.
- **Milestone L20:** *"Word gets around."*

### Levels 21–30 · Italian Service  *(stage: Fridge & Freshness)*
- **L21 — Refrigerator & freshness:** the day now opens with its card and
  ends with Closing Time; food ages, can spoil; the fridge has
  a capacity. Throw out expired food in Inventory. Menu guests: 2 per
  service.
- **Techniques:** **julienne** L21, **radial** cuts L23.
- **Buy:** **Growing Kitchen** ($16,000, L21 — +1.5% quality on order
  earnings). Nakiri (L25, $700). Bread Knife & Marble Board (L30,
  $850 / $750).

### Levels 31–40 · French Bistro  *(stage: Dine-in Service)*
- **L31 — Dine-in service and supplies:** each order needs a clean **place
  setting** (plate, fork, knife — washed after every service) and a
  **napkin**; **dish soap** for washing up, **cleaning liquid** at closing.
  Buy them in **Market → Supplies**.
- **Techniques:** **rock-mince** L34, **chiffonade** L35.
- **L40:** Cleaver ($1,100), Dark Oak Board ($1,000).

### Levels 41–50 · French Precision  *(stage: Staff)*
- **L41 — Staff:** busy services need a team. **Prep Cook** (L41, from 3
  orders), **Server** (L46, from 2 tables). The check names who you need;
  **hiring is free** and no wages are charged until Level 91.
- **Buy:** **Established Kitchen** ($20,000, L41 — +3% quality),
  **Quality Chef** helper ($6,000, L45).
- **Milestone L45:** *"Something worth keeping."*
- **L50:** Damascus Knife ($1,800), Chef's Copper Board ($1,500).

### Levels 51–70 · Indian Kitchen · Indian Aromatics & Service  *(stage: Cuisines & Specialists)*
- **L51 — New cuisines:** each brings its **specialist chef** (hire free).
  **Indian Chef** at L51. **Breakfast** joins the day (3 services).
  Menu guests: 3 per service.
- **Buy:** **Neighborhood Café** ($21,000, L51 — +4% quality, +1 guest
  seat).
- **L61 — Line Cook** needed (from 4 orders).
- **L65:** **Kitchen Assistant** helper ($4,000).
- **Milestone L70:** *"The last wish."*

### Levels 71–90 · Mediterranean & Levant · Mexican & Latin Kitchen  *(stage: Takeaway)*
- **L71 — Takeaway:** ~30% of orders are takeaway — each needs a
  **container + bag** (Market → Supplies). **Mediterranean Chef** (L71).
- **Buy:** **Flourishing Café** ($24,000, L71 — +5% quality, +1 seat).
- **L81 — Mexican** cuisine (**Mexican Chef**).
- **L90:** Obsidian Knife ($2,200).

### Levels 91–120 · Latin Service → Japanese Kitchen & Precision  *(stage: Bigger Restaurant)*
- **L91 — A bigger restaurant:** the day ends with the full **End
  Business Day** — **wages are now paid** (staff + specialists), the
  inspection, the day's P&L. **Cleaner** needed (L91).
- **Buy:** **Grand Kitchen** ($29,000, L91 — +7% quality, +2 seats).
- **L100 — The finale** story sequence plays.
- **L101 — Japanese** (one **Asian Chef** covers the 4 Asian cuisines);
  **meat** arrives (L101), **ribeye** (L106), **fish** (L109).
- **Boards:** Butcher's Block (L106, $1,800), Seafood Slate (L109, $2,000).
- **Milestone L110:** *"The kitchen grows."*
- **Milestone L120:** *"Grand Service — the kitchen you built runs itself."*

### Levels 121–160 · Chinese Kitchen & Wok · Thai & Southeast Asian  *(stage: Full Management)*
- **L121 — Full restaurant management:** **Head Chef** needed (with 3+
  specialist cuisines). **Chinese** (L121), **Thai** (L141). Menu guests: 4.

### Levels 161–200 · Korean Kitchen · Italian, French & Indian Grand Services  *(stage: Established Restaurant)*
- **L161 — Korean** cuisine; **Manager** needed (team of 5+).
  High-volume service, careful stock planning — use **Market → Plan
  ahead** (Today / 2 days / 3 days).

### Levels 201–240 · Mediterranean Encore · Latin Mastery · Japanese-Korean & Chinese-Thai Fusion  *(stage: Master Restaurant)*
- Almost everything runs at once. Menu guests: 5 per service.

### Levels 241–250 · The Grand Finale  *(stage: Grand Service)*
- The last dishes; **Level 250 = Grand Service**.
- **Rewards:** the **Family Legacy $50,000** final reward, *"Campaign
  complete"* story beat, **#1 in the city ranking**.
- **After L250:** the **Endless Restaurant** opens — run full business days
  with events (Dinner Rush, Large Group), Today's Special and up to 3
  stars a day.

### Everyday stock  *(Market)*
- **Ingredients** (from L15): 57 ingredients, in lb or kg (Settings),
  ¼ steps; bulk discounts 3/5/8% from 25/50/100. **Supplier** (Restaurant
  → Suppliers): Local, Wholesale (cheapest) or Premium (+1 fresh day, +2%
  quality).
- **Supplies** (from L31): plates, cutlery, napkins, dish soap, cleaning
  liquid; takeaway containers and bags (from L71).
- **In a hurry:** Quick Restock in the Pre-Service Check (+25%). **Broke?**
  Grandma's pantry lends what a service needs (no quality bonus that time).

## How you progress

- **City ranking:** you start **#50 of 50** restaurants and pass a rival
  every 2–7 levels; **#1 exactly at Level 250**.
- **Kitchen title** (café rank): Humble Kitchen → Growing Kitchen (L10) →
  Morning Café (L20) → Working Kitchen (L30) → Bakery Corner (L40) →
  Neighborhood Café (L50) → … → Grand Service (L120).
- **Money:** each level pays its orders + a completion reward, plus 22
  milestone rewards ($38,200 in all) and the $50,000 Family Legacy. A
  player who buys everything still ends Level 250 with about **$175,000**.
- **Never stuck:** no debt, no fail state — Grandma's pantry and free
  hiring always let a service start.
