KnifeCraft — Changes Made During This Chat
==========================================

This file summarizes the game and campaign work completed in this chat, including
the final audio-policy fix. The current work is on branch codex/restaurant-map.

KITCHEN AND DAILY ROUTINES
--------------------------
- Added a separate Daily Maintenance flow for the checks players need before
  service, with day and night cleaning handled as separate routines.
- Expanded the checks to cover ingredient stock and urgent/low-stock refills,
  cutlery and culinary supplies, cleaning supplies, kitchen equipment, tools
  needed at the current level, staff and chef hiring needs, and refrigerator
  capacity.
- Added a Daily Check shortcut on the Kitchen screen.
- Reduced the space used by the Daily Check and Today's Order cards, made the
  order details action easier to see, and made the order card more compact.
- Removed duplicate progress and Market shortcuts from the Kitchen overview.
- Moved Kitchen Upgrade out of the Kitchen overview into its own Business
  section.
- Added compact expandable shortcut buttons for Daily Check, Inventory, and
  Business, with status indicators for areas needing attention.

RESTAURANT AND BUSINESS UI
--------------------------
- Updated the Restaurant section containers and surrounding screen background
  to use the game's cream-colored visual theme.
- Added a cross-screen attention system for actionable issues including cleaning,
  restroom supplies, dining-area cleanup, inventory refills, hiring, equipment,
  refrigerator capacity, and cleaning supplies.
- Replaced plain numeric attention badges with issue-appropriate symbols or
  emoji indicators where the UI presents those alerts.
- Updated Business dashboard sections and related presentation for the overview,
  equipment, staff, suppliers, menu, and operations areas.
- Updated shared money/cash presentation in the affected UI components.

ONBOARDING, STOCK, AND SERVICE FLOW
-----------------------------------
- Removed the free Grandma's ingredient stock path from the opening campaign.
- Made the Market available early and moved ingredient purchasing into the
  campaign onboarding, starting around level 3.
- Updated first-restock, campaign-stock, pre-service, service-cover, supplies,
  cleanliness, tool, and staff progression rules to follow the campaign's
  intended unlock sequence.
- Updated the first-use inventory and recipe presentation so service checks are
  based on the actual recipe requirements.

150-LEVEL CAMPAIGN REDESIGN
--------------------------
- Curated 150 retained levels from the former 250-level catalog and re-numbered
  them sequentially from Level 1 through Level 150.
- Reworked the opening training level and early recipes, including a no-cost
  basic-skills practice lesson, fixed recipe choice at the early Italian toast
  level, and clarified the potato preparation sequence.
- Updated chapter names and chapter mapping, technique and ingredient unlocks,
  recipe selection, campaign milestones, story finale, and campaign rewards.
- Re-mapped progression thresholds for business access, cuisines, tools, staff,
  cleaning, service supplies, menu systems, and other systems that previously
  referenced the 250-level campaign.
- Added one-time save migration for the revised campaign. Retained levels map to
  their new numbers; removed levels transfer only when their recipe pool and
  service shape match; a completed old Level 250 maps to a completed new
  campaign.
- Added a campaign audit generator and generated level maps, recipe quality
  ledger, ingredient/fruit/technique coverage plans, and campaign catalog under
  docs/campaign-redesign-150/.
- Static campaign audit: 150 selected levels, 150 unique source levels, 168
  recipes, 57 ingredients, and 15 fruits; no missing ingredient or fruit
  references; technique unlocks match first use.

RUNTIME FIXES
-------------
- Fixed the campaign migration import so it reads CAMPAIGN_150_SOURCE_LEVEL_NUMBERS
  from campaignSelection.ts, the module that exports it. This resolves the
  missing-export exception that previously left the app on a white screen.
- Updated Prepared status checks to account for both recipe-pool and batch-group
  levels.
- Added a React error-boundary fallback screen with a reload action for render
  failures.
- Fixed Web Audio startup behavior: sound effects and music now wait for a player
  gesture before creating an AudioContext. Resume attempts are shared and handled
  safely; closed or unavailable audio contexts no longer break the game screen.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS

  react-refresh/only-export-components warnings in UI component files.
- git diff --check completed without whitespace errors.
- Per repository instructions, tests, builds, and the development server were
  not run.
- Campaign economy simulation was not run.
